Surf Fit · 40-Minute Sessions

Sessions & Descriptions

Three sessions a week built around what surfing asks of you: paddle endurance, an explosive pop-up, and rotational core and balance. Kit needed — one 20 kg kettlebell, two 10 kg dumbbells, a mat.

How each session runs

15-min run first to get warm, then ~2 min of movement prep, a ~20 min circuit, and a short core finisher — in and out inside 40 minutes.

Rounds: aim for 4 circuit rounds, but run 3 if the clock is tight — quality reps beat rushing. Rest ~60 seconds between rounds.

Progressing: your weights are fixed, so add a round, slow the tempo, or shorten the rest before you worry about load.
