Club hire at every course. Each course here rents complete sets — mention right- or left-handed and men's or ladies' when you book, and reserve the set together with your tee time. Alternatively, an Algarve golf-delivery service can drop a hire set at your apartment for the whole trip. Green fees swing a lot by season and time of day (twilight rates are cheaper), so check current prices on each course's own page.
Getting there. With clubs and a bag, a car or taxi is realistically the way to every course — public transport isn't practical for golf. The distances and one-way Uber estimates below are measured from Praça Luís de Camões, so they double as a rough taxi guide; real fares move with demand and time of day. Boavista, Palmares and Espiche are short hops; the Portimão, Carvoeiro and Silves courses are a longer run east. Where a course has its own official site, the card links to it, and every card has a Google Maps pin.
