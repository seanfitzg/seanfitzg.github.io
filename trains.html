Dublin Bike instead of the Luas: cycling between Heuston and a stand near Dawson or Molesworth St takes about 20 min including docking, against about 30 min by Luas. That saves about 10 min on every Heuston journey: arrive at work 10 min earlier in the morning, or leave work 10 min later in the evening. From Pearse the walk is only about 800 m, so a bike saves nothing. Check stand availability in the Dublin Bikes app at peak times.
