Swing
Thoughts

Rhythm above all.

Never force the swing to add distance — the moment you press for it, consistency goes. Let speed come from a smooth, complete motion.

In-swing thought (feel it as you go) Target (aiming for, not thinking about)

Grip

Left handBase of the fingers.
Right handOnly the fingers.
  • Light grip at all times.

Setup Routine

  1. Stand behind the ball. Pick the target line and an intermediate spot a foot or two ahead of the ball to aim at.
  2. Place right foot opposite the ball, perpendicular to the target line. Use left foot for driver.Square — a clean reference before flaring.
  3. Sole the club behind the ball, face square to the intermediate spot.
  4. Get both feet into position — ball position set by the lead foot, trail foot out to stance width for the club.
  5. Flare left foot just past comfortable, pivoting from the toe so the heel stays put.Keeps the aim you just set.
  6. Check alignment off the heels, not the toes — heels parallel to the target line. Aim with your left shoulder.
  7. Posture: bend from the waist (not too much, not too straight), sit onto the hips / bum out so the back is straight, minimal knee bend, stand as close to the ball as you can. Relaxed right arm.
  8. One waggle to keep the hands soft, then go.

Backswing

Start slow — set the rhythm here.

  • Start SLOW and straight back at the very start.
  • Keep the angle between the right forearm and clubshaft constant for the first 12–24 inches.
  • Keep the clubhead “closed.” Face angle matches spine angle.
  • Once the clubhead is horizontal, feel like the hands go UP.
  • Keep hands & arms in front of the body at all times. Don’t come inside.
  • Make a full shoulder and hip turn.

Transition

Slow here for good rhythm.

  • Start the downswing by turning the hips (some days the left knee).
  • Keep the left bum back as the hips turn — keep the body angles, don’t early extend.
  • Feel wristy at the transition.

Downswing

Rotate through — full, complete finish.

  • Turn hips and shoulders. Rotate the body through the downswing.
  • Don’t drop the right elbow — let it swing naturally.
  • Don’t drop the right shoulder. It may feel more powerful, but (per SwingCaddy) you lose distance. Downswing by turning the upper body.

Wedges

  • Turn everything back and forward at the same time.
  • Start the downswing by turning the hips.

Chipping

  • Stand tall.
  • Grip lightly.
  • Weight on the front foot.
  • Stand close to the ball.
  • Hit down on the ball.
All grip & setup thoughts applied every time. Backswing: 1–2 thoughts · Transition: 1 thought.
