About the numbers. Distances are approximate road distances from central Lagos (Praça Luís de Camões); drive times assume normal traffic. Uber figures are rough one-way UberX estimates for planning only — real fares move with demand and time of day. For the far day-trips, a hire car or a tour with its own transfer is usually cheaper and calmer than round-trip Ubers.
Getting around without a car. Lagos sits at the western end of the CP train line — quick, inexpensive runs east to Portimão, Silves and beyond (tickets at cp.pt). Vamus regional buses head west to Sagres and Aljezur and up to Monchique (change at Portimão); a Tourist Pass gives unlimited Vamus travel for about €35 (3 days) or €45 (7 days). In town, the little "A Onda" minibuses reach the beaches, and the scenic boat trips leave from Lagos Marina, a short stroll from the square. A few stations (Silves, Estômbar) sit outside their towns, so a short taxi finishes the trip. Where a place has its own official site, the card links to it.
