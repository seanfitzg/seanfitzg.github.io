About the numbers. Distances are approximate road distances from central Faro; drive times assume normal traffic. Uber figures are rough one-way UberX estimates for planning only — real fares swing with demand, time of day and surge. Uber availability thins out west of Albufeira and for early-morning tour pickups, so for the day-trip band a rental car or the tour operator's own transfer is often cheaper and more reliable.
Getting around without a car. The Algarve is unusually doable by public transport. Two networks cover almost everything here: the CP train (Linha do Algarve) runs the coast from Lagos through Faro to Vila Real de Santo António and is cheap — Faro→Albufeira is about €3.40, Faro→Lagos about €7.30 (buy at cp.pt). Vamus regional buses fill in the towns off the tracks; a Tourist Pass gives unlimited Vamus rides for about €35 (3 days) or €45 (7 days). Around Faro, local Próximo buses run to the airport and beach. Two things to know: several stations (Loulé, Albufeira, Silves, Alcantarilha) sit well outside the towns they name, so a bus or short taxi finishes the trip; and Google Maps has both networks built in for live times. Where an attraction has its own official site, the card links straight to it — towns, beaches and monuments without a dedicated site are left unlinked.
