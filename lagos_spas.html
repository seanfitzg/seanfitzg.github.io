A spa afternoon, booked in advance.

Six resort spas that welcome day visitors — four within minutes of the old town, two worth the drive.

6 spas4 in & around Lagosday passes · treatments
Before you go. At resort spas the wet areas (pool, sauna, Turkish bath) usually need a booked day pass, and massages and treatments are extra and booked separately — so call or reserve ahead, ideally a day or two out. Access policies shift with the season and how full the hotel is; a couple of these have at times limited the spa to staying guests, so it's always worth confirming when you book. Prices aren't listed here as they change often — check each spa's own page (linked below) for current rates.

≈3–5 km · a short taxi from the square
5-star · clifftop

Cascade Spa — Cascade Wellness Resort

The headline choice: a wellness-focused five-star perched on the Ponta da Piedade cliffs, with a heated outdoor pool, sauna, jacuzzi, steam room and ice fountain, plus a full treatment menu. It runs a one-day spa programme for non-guests — though access has at times been kept for hotel guests only, so confirm when you book.

Beachfront · Meia Praia

Satsanga Spa — Vila Galé Lagos

A stylish hotel right on Meia Praia, with the Satsanga spa: an indoor panoramic pool, sauna, Turkish bath and hydromassage, plus relaxation and anti-stress treatments. Book the circuit or a treatment through the spa reception; the wet areas are for ages 16 and over.

4-star · Porto de Mós

Belmar Spa & Beach Resort

A design-led resort above Porto de Mós beach, five minutes from the centre. The Belmar Spa has an indoor pool, sauna, Turkish bath and a vichy shower, alongside a broad menu of massages, body treatments and facials. Reserve spa access or a treatment ahead.

Spa — Iberostar Selection Lagos Algarve

A contemporary five-star by Meia Praia with a modern spa — Turkish bath and a menu of treatments in an intimate setting, plus several pools including a heated one. Day access is more limited here, so the surest way in is to book a treatment.

Alma Spa — Monte Santo Resort

The clearest bet for a booked day out: this Carvoeiro resort spa openly sells non-guest day passes to its wellness centre — heated indoor pool, sauna, Turkish bath and jacuzzi — and offers Thalgo facial and body treatments. Combine it with the Carvoeiro boardwalk on the ladies' guide.

Monchique mountains · thermal

Thermal Spa — Villa Termal Caldas de Monchique

The Algarve's only thermal spa, in a wooded mountain village — alkaline spring waters, a thermal pool, saunas and treatments in a genuinely tranquil setting. Day spa programmes are bookable, making a restorative half-day paired with a Monchique lunch. (Also on the ladies' guide.)
