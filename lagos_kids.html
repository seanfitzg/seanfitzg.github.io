Ten days in Lagos, sorted by how far you'll travel.

Sea caves, water parks, go-karts, cliff-top kayaking and the wild west coast — all genuinely fun for 12–14 year olds. Grouped by distance from central Lagos (Praça Luís de Camões), each with a one-way Uber estimate, a public-transport option, and an official link where one exists.

About the numbers. Distances are approximate road distances from central Lagos; drive times assume normal traffic. Uber figures are rough one-way UberX estimates for planning only — real fares swing with demand, time of day and surge. For the far day-trips a rental car or the tour operator's own transfer is often cheaper and more reliable than round-trip Ubers.
Getting around without a car. Lagos is the western end of the CP train line (fast, cheap trips east to Portimão, Silves, Albufeira and Faro — buy at cp.pt). Vamus regional buses run west to Sagres and Aljezur and up to Monchique (via Portimão), and a Tourist Pass gives unlimited Vamus rides for about €35 (3 days) / €45 (7 days). Around town, the small "A Onda" minibuses reach the beaches and the zoo, and most boat trips leave straight from Lagos Marina, a short walk from the centre. Google Maps has both the train and bus networks built in for live times. Where an attraction has its own official site, the card links to it.
